## Interpretation boundary

The comparison supports an **observed temporal and cross-surface improvement** relative to earlier screenshots where the project was absent or interpreted generically.

The evidence does not identify which individual intervention caused the change. Possible contributors include the public Entity Home, Vercel deployment, Blogger updates, GitHub/RAG content, indexing delay and the revised definition. These are candidate contributors, not individually tested causes.

The correct claim is therefore:

> AIO CODE and Marii Cuadros show an observed improvement in retrieval, project recognition and partial entity disambiguation in the 2026-09-29/30 screenshots compared with earlier baseline screenshots. The result is not yet a stability or generalization claim; T+7 observation remains pending.
