# Ecosystem Continuity & Recovery Map

## Objective

Ensure that loss, restriction or compromise of one platform does not destroy the identity, history or operational continuity of the ecosystem.

## Recovery layers

### Layer 1 — Account

Protect access with platform-native security controls, strong unique credentials, multi-factor authentication, recovery methods and login alerts.

### Layer 2 — Canonical identity

Maintain the canonical Entity Home and stable entity IDs so that platform changes do not redefine the entities.

### Layer 3 — Content

Keep structured content records and private originals independently of social platforms.

### Layer 4 — Infrastructure

Maintain copies of critical schemas, registries, documentation and deployment configuration outside any single platform dependency.

### Layer 5 — Independent backup

Maintain at least one independent backup that is not continuously connected to the primary working environment.

## Recovery principle

A platform account is a surface of the ecosystem, not the ecosystem itself.

## Sensitive material

Recovery codes, passwords, private contact information and identity documents are never stored in this public repository.
