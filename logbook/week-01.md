## Week 1 Overview

The first week of AIO CODE focused on establishing the initial infrastructure for the experiment.

The main objective was to investigate whether a previously fragmented digital presence could be organized into a more coherent representation of a single digital entity across multiple public platforms.

During this period, the project focused on:

- Auditing the existing digital presence of Marii Cuadros.
- Identifying active digital nodes.
- Establishing a consistent identity representation.
- Designing an initial Schema.org / JSON-LD structure.
- Creating the first technical repositories.
- Establishing the initial documentation architecture.

This stage represents the **baseline and infrastructure phase** of the experiment.

---

# Day 1 — Digital Entity Audit and Initial Node Mapping

## Objective

Audit the existing digital presence associated with the entity **Marii Cuadros** and identify potential inconsistencies between platforms.

## Actions Performed

### 1. Digital Identity Audit

The existing presence of the entity **Marii Cuadros** was reviewed across publicly accessible digital platforms.

The initial audit included profiles and assets associated with:

- GitHub
- Medium
- Hugging Face
- Pinterest
- Spotify
- Other active social platforms

The purpose was to determine how consistently the same person was represented across different digital environments.

### 2. Entity Attribute Review

The following identity attributes were considered during the audit:

- Name
- Alternate names
- Professional identity
- Areas of activity
- Platform profiles
- Relationships between digital nodes

### 3. Initial Schema.org Representation

An initial `Person` representation using Schema.org and JSON-LD was created.

The structure included properties such as:

- `name`
- `alternateName`
- `description`
- `sameAs`
- `knowsAbout`
- `nationality`
- `homeLocation`

The initial schema was treated as an experimental representation of the entity rather than as proof that the entity would subsequently be recognized by search engines or AI systems.

---

## Observation

The audit showed that information about Marii Cuadros was distributed across multiple platforms and that the representation of the entity was not completely standardized between all nodes.

This created the initial research opportunity for AIO CODE:

> Could a more consistent representation of the same entity across multiple public nodes improve the ability of automated systems to associate those nodes with one person?

---

## Technical Hypothesis

AIO CODE initially hypothesized that explicit relationships between profiles, including the use of `sameAs`, could potentially facilitate the association of multiple public representations belonging to the same entity.

This hypothesis was not considered proven during Week 1 and required further experimentation.

# Day 2 — Blogger Configuration and Initial Web Hub

## Objective

Establish a primary web publication environment capable of functioning as a central public hub for the project and its documentation.

## Actions Performed

### 1. Blogger Infrastructure

A Blogger site was configured as part of the AIO CODE ecosystem.

The site was intended to provide a publicly accessible web location where information about the project and the entity could be published.

### 2. Static Information Structure

Initial pages and content structures were created to establish the identity and purpose of the project.

### 3. Structured Data Experiment

Initial JSON-LD / structured-data implementation was introduced into the Blogger environment.

The intention was to experiment with explicit machine-readable relationships between:

- Marii Cuadros
- AIO CODE
- Project information
- Public web content

---

## Observation

The creation of a dedicated web hub introduced a primary public node that could be connected to the other digital assets of the experiment.

At this stage, no causal conclusion was made regarding whether the structured data itself would influence indexing or AI recognition.

---

## Technical Hypothesis

AIO CODE hypothesized that a consistent web hub containing structured and human-readable information could provide a clearer representation of the relationship between the creator, the project and its associated content.

Further observation would be required to determine whether this produced measurable changes.

# Week 1 — Initial Findings

At the end of the first week, several preliminary findings emerged.

### Finding 1 — Digital identity was distributed

The identity of Marii Cuadros existed across multiple platforms rather than within a single centralized digital source.

### Finding 2 — Platform consistency was incomplete

Different platforms contained different descriptions, contexts and representations of the same person.

### Finding 3 — Explicit relationships could be tested

The presence of multiple public profiles created an opportunity to experimentally test whether explicit relationships between nodes could influence entity association or retrieval.

### Finding 4 — Structured data could be experimentally evaluated

Schema.org and JSON-LD were incorporated as experimental tools for representing the entity in a machine-readable format.

### Finding 5 — AIO CODE required a controlled documentation system

The experiment generated enough variables and interventions to require chronological documentation.

This led to the establishment of the GitHub logbook and schema structure.

---

# Week 1 — Working Hypotheses

The following hypotheses were established for subsequent experimentation:

### H1 — Entity Consistency

A consistent representation of a person's identity across multiple public platforms may facilitate association between those digital nodes.

### H2 — Explicit Relationships

Explicit relationships such as `sameAs` may contribute to the association of different public profiles with the same entity.

### H3 — Structured Representation

Machine-readable structured data may provide additional explicit information about an entity that can potentially be used by systems capable of processing such data.

### H4 — Distributed Presence

A coherent network of public digital nodes may produce different discovery and retrieval behavior than isolated profiles.

None of these hypotheses were considered proven at the end of Week 1.

---

# Methodological Rule Established

A central methodological rule was established during the first week:

> **An observed change should not automatically be interpreted as proof of causation.**

AIO CODE therefore distinguishes between:

- Observation
- Hypothesis
- Experiment
- Result
- Interpretation
- Conclusion

This distinction becomes increasingly important as the experiment progresses.
