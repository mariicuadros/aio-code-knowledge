# AIO CODE — Ethics

**Version:** 1.1  
**Status:** Active  
**Updated:** 2026-09-03

## Purpose

Ethics is the research-integrity and responsible-practice layer of AIO CODE. It governs how observations, experiments, measurements, interpretations, findings and interventions are documented and communicated.

Ethics does not replace evidence. It defines the conditions for handling evidence responsibly.

## Research Architecture

```text
ENTITY
   ↓
OBSERVATORY
   ↓
ENTITY LABS
   ↓
METRICS
   ↓
ETHICS
   ↓
FINDINGS
```

- **Entity** — defines what is being studied.
- **Observatory** — records what systems produce.
- **Entity Labs** — investigates research questions through experiments.
- **Metrics** — measures and compares changes.
- **Ethics** — governs evidence, integrity, uncertainty and claims.
- **Findings** — communicates conclusions supported by the research record.

## Evidence Levels

- **Observed** — directly recorded under defined conditions.
- **Corroborated** — supported by multiple independent observations, systems, measurements or sources.
- **Verified** — sufficiently supported for the specific claim being evaluated.
- **Hypothesized** — proposed explanation or expected relationship not sufficiently established.
- **Unknown** — insufficient evidence.

```text
Observed Fact
     ≠
Corroborated Evidence
     ≠
Verified Finding
     ≠
Interpretation
     ≠
Hypothesis
     ≠
Causal Conclusion
```

An established observation must not be downgraded to a hypothesis merely because its mechanism remains unknown. Conversely, an observed change must not be presented as proof of causation without sufficient evidence.

## Research Integrity

AIO CODE must:

- preserve original observations and measurements;
- preserve historical research states;
- distinguish raw observations from derived measurements and interpretations;
- preserve negative, failed and inconclusive results;
- maintain traceability across entity, observation, experiment and measurement records;
- document interventions and testing conditions;
- avoid fabricated evidence, citations or system outputs;
- keep uncertainty visible;
- protect unnecessary personal information;
- communicate claims proportionally to evidence.

## Causality

```text
Change observed
       ≠
Intervention caused the change
```

Temporal association, correlation or repeated observation does not automatically establish causation.

## Entity Integrity

The canonical entity must remain distinct from competing representations.

```text
MC-001 — Marii Cuadros
AIO-001 — AIO CODE
```

An observed competing representation such as **Maria Luisa Cuadros** belongs in the research record as an observation, not in the canonical identity.

## Intervention Boundaries

Interventions must be legitimate, documented, attributable, measurable and proportionate. AIO CODE must not intentionally rely on deception, fraud, unauthorized access, abuse or fabricated identities/evidence.

## Responsible Publication

Research communication should state, where relevant:

- what was observed;
- what was corroborated;
- what was verified;
- what was interpreted;
- what was hypothesized;
- what remains unknown;
- and what limitations apply.

## Core Rule

> **Evidence determines the strength of the claim.**

> **Identity should be established before visibility is optimized.**

> **A measured change does not automatically establish causation.**
