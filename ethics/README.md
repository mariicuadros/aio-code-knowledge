# AIO CODE — Ethics

## Purpose

Ethics is the research integrity and responsible-practice layer of AIO CODE.

It defines the principles governing how observations, experiments, measurements, conclusions, interventions, and published research artifacts are created, interpreted, documented, and communicated.

Ethics does not replace evidence.

It establishes the conditions under which evidence should be collected, interpreted, and communicated responsibly.

---

## Research Architecture

AIO CODE follows this research sequence:

```text
ENTITY
   ↓
OBSERVATORY
   ↓
ENTITY LABS
   ↓
METRICS
   ↓
ETHICS
````

Each layer has a distinct function:

```text
Entity
    → Defines what the entity is.

Observatory
    → Records what systems do.

Entity Labs
    → Investigates defined research questions through experiments.

Metrics
    → Measures and compares observed changes.

Ethics
    → Defines how research evidence, interventions, conclusions, and limitations are handled responsibly.
```

---

## Evidence Levels

AIO CODE distinguishes different levels of research evidence.

```text
Observed
    ↓
Corroborated
    ↓
Verified
```

These states must not be treated as interchangeable.

---

## Observed

An observation is a directly recorded result obtained under defined conditions.

Example:

```text
A system returned a specific entity association.
```

An observed result is evidence of what occurred under the recorded conditions.

It does not automatically establish why it occurred.

---

## Corroborated

A result may be considered corroborated when the same phenomenon is supported by multiple independent observations, systems, measurements, or sources.

Corroboration increases confidence but does not automatically establish causation.

---

## Verified

A result may be classified as verified when the available evidence and methodology provide sufficient support for the specific claim being made.

Verification is always claim-specific.

A verified observation does not mean that every possible explanation of that observation has been verified.

---

## Hypothesis

A hypothesis is a proposed explanation or expected relationship that has not yet been sufficiently established.

AIO CODE must not describe an untested hypothesis as an observed fact.

Example:

```text
Observation:
Google Search associated a query with another entity.

Hypothesis:
A specific identity signal may influence that behavior.

Conclusion:
Not established until sufficient evidence exists.
```

---

## Evidence Separation

AIO CODE maintains the distinction:

```text
Observed Fact
     ≠
Corroborated Evidence
     ≠
Verified Finding
     ≠
Interpretation
     ≠
Hypothesis
     ≠
Causal Conclusion
```

This distinction is mandatory throughout the research architecture.

---

## Existing Research Evidence

AIO CODE has already generated observations and structured research artifacts across multiple stages of the project.

These include:

* canonical entity definitions
* entity relationships
* structured claims
* entity-resolution observations
* distributed source architecture
* observations across AI and search systems
* documented retrieval and representation behavior
* structured research datasets
* experimental architecture
* measurement architecture

These artifacts should be treated according to their actual evidence status.

AIO CODE must not downgrade established observations to hypotheses simply because their underlying mechanisms remain under investigation.

At the same time, an observed outcome must not be presented as proof of a causal mechanism that has not been demonstrated.

---

## Causality

AIO CODE does not infer causation from correlation alone.

The following distinction must be preserved:

```text
Change observed
        ≠
Intervention caused the change
```

A causal conclusion requires appropriate experimental evidence and control of relevant alternative explanations.

---

## Research Transparency

AIO CODE should document:

* what was observed
* when it was observed
* where it was observed
* which system was evaluated
* which query or prompt was used
* which conditions were present
* what intervention was applied
* what changed
* what did not change
* what remains unknown

Unfavorable or unexpected results must not be removed simply because they contradict the expected outcome.

---

## Reproducibility

Whenever practical, research observations and experiments should contain enough information for another researcher to understand how the result was obtained.

Relevant information may include:

* entity
* system
* query
* prompt
* language
* environment
* date
* intervention
* measurement method
* result
* evidence level

---

## Historical Integrity

Historical research records should be preserved.

If a previous interpretation changes because new evidence becomes available, the new interpretation should be documented without rewriting history.

The research record should preserve:

```text
What was believed at the time
        ↓
What evidence existed at the time
        ↓
What later evidence changed
        ↓
What is currently supported
```

---

## Intervention Boundaries

AIO CODE may investigate how changes in digital information architecture affect the recognition, retrieval, resolution, representation, citation, or recommendation of defined entities.

Interventions should be:

* documented
* attributable
* reversible when practical
* measurable
* limited to legitimate research purposes

AIO CODE should not intentionally manipulate systems through deceptive, fraudulent, abusive, or unauthorized methods.

---

## No Fabricated Evidence

AIO CODE must never:

* fabricate observations
* fabricate measurements
* fabricate citations
* fabricate system responses
* present simulated results as real results
* convert expected outcomes into observed outcomes
* claim verification without sufficient evidence

When evidence is unavailable, the appropriate state is:

```text
Unknown
```

---

## Negative and Unexpected Results

Negative results are valid research results.

AIO CODE preserves:

* no change
* unexpected change
* negative change
* inconsistent behavior
* failed intervention
* inconclusive result
* insufficient evidence

Research quality is not determined by whether an intervention produces the expected result.

---

## Entity Integrity

The canonical identity of an entity must be clearly distinguished from competing, ambiguous, or incorrect representations.

For example:

```text
Canonical Entity:
MC-001 — Marii Cuadros

Competing Representation:
Maria Luisa Cuadros
```

An observed association with another representation should be documented as an entity-resolution phenomenon rather than silently incorporated into the canonical identity.

---

## AI System Integrity

When evaluating AI systems, AIO CODE should distinguish:

```text
System Output
     ↓
Observed Representation
     ↓
Interpretation
```

An AI-generated statement is evidence of the system's output under the recorded conditions.

It is not automatically an authoritative statement about reality.

---

## Search System Integrity

Search results are treated as observations of a retrieval and representation environment.

Search ranking, associations, snippets, panels, recommendations, and retrieved sources may change over time.

Therefore, measurements should include temporal and environmental context whenever relevant.

---

## Privacy and Personal Information

AIO CODE should avoid unnecessary collection, publication, or distribution of sensitive personal information.

Research artifacts should contain only information necessary for the defined research objective.

---

## Responsible Publication

Research findings should communicate:

* evidence
* methodology
* limitations
* uncertainty
* competing explanations
* confidence level

Claims should be proportional to the strength of the evidence supporting them.

---

## Research Principle

AIO CODE follows the principle:

> Evidence determines the strength of the claim.

And:

> Identity should be established before visibility is optimized.

And:

> A measured change does not automatically establish causation.

---

## Ethics and the Research Pipeline

Ethics applies across the complete AIO CODE pipeline:

```text
ENTITY
   ↓
OBSERVATION
   ↓
EXPERIMENT
   ↓
MEASUREMENT
   ↓
RESULT
   ↓
INTERPRETATION
   ↓
CONCLUSION
```

At every stage, the evidence level and limitations must remain explicit.
