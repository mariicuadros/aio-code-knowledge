## Purpose

This document defines the non-negotiable creative rules for MC-001 content so that platform optimization, commercial integrations and experimentation do not erase the artistic identity of Marii Cuadros or distort VOID MODE.

## Core principles

1. **No niche lock.** Marii Cuadros is not reduced to fitness, beauty, AI, music, fashion, psychology, spirituality or any single category. Those are dimensions of one artistic entity.
2. **Artist first.** AI, algorithms, platforms and tools support the work; they are not the identity of the artist.
3. **Human perspective remains visible.** Maria's real-world voice, reactions, contradictions and observations anchor the cinematic universe.
4. **VOID MODE shows; Maria interprets.** Symbolic/cinematic material should primarily express experience through story and image. Direct explanation belongs mainly to real-world/log/lifestyle formats.
5. **Narrative complexity, surface clarity.** A first-time viewer should receive a complete emotional or conceptual unit; returning viewers may detect deeper continuity.
6. **No saturation.** Publishing volume must not erase coherence, mystery or meaning.
7. **No deletion for weak performance alone.** Low-performing assets remain evidence. Removal requires a deliberate legal, safety, rights, privacy, platform or archival reason.
8. **No false commercial relationship.** Brand presence, props, clothing or product use must not imply payment, authorization, endorsement or partnership unless it exists and is documented.
9. **External people are not growth props.** Real third parties enter content only by deliberate creative/commercial decision with appropriate permissions and context.
10. **Psychology, faith and astrology coexist as narrative lenses.** Personal experience, symbolism and belief must not be silently presented as clinical or scientific fact.
11. **Transformation is the core narrative.** The multiverse externalizes internal change rather than existing only as spectacle.
12. **Ambiguity is intentional, confusion is not.** Mystery should create questions, not destroy basic comprehension.

## Platform grammar

### TikTok

Primary role: discovery, search, sound-led retention and personality.

- Micro-scenes may begin with a 0–3 second dialogue/question/conflict hook before music enters.
- Trailers may intentionally omit explanatory context.
- Every piece should leave a clear emotional impression or open question.
- Lifestyle/log content may explicitly mention AIO CODE and VOID MODE when natural.

### Instagram

Primary role: canon, psychological/spiritual/symbolic narrative, visual identity and brand-facing proof.

- Canon structure may use real vertical opening → glitch/transition → horizontal cinematic sequence → real-world reaction/loop closure.
- Symbolism should carry meaning without requiring explanatory exposition inside the cinematic sequence.
- Carrusels and Stories expand character, editorial, early-access and real-world creator context.

### Facebook

Primary role: human/community perspective, Frank voice and selected multiverse distribution.

### Pinterest

Primary role: editorial archive and magazine-style visual world-building, not a repost dump.

### YouTube / Bilibili

Primary role: documentary depth, process, creation, explanation and educational material; Shorts may derive from documentary/process assets.

## Commercial integration rule

Commercial readiness is designed into the ecosystem, but individual posts do not need to sell. Brand objects may appear organically or as speculative creative, but the relationship must always be correctly classified in AIO CODE records.

## Final rule

The audience receives a coherent artistic experience. AIO CODE receives structured evidence, provenance, rights context, semantic intent and performance observations behind that experience.
