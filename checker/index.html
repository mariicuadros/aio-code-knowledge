Entity readiness check

Review the records and processes behind a public digital identity. This checklist helps surface documentation gaps so you can decide what to improve next.

Important: This is a creator-entered self-assessment. It does not query ChatGPT, Google, or other AI/search systems, verify your answers, issue a score, or predict visibility. It is not a legal clearance.
Answers stay in this page session. This prototype sends no form data and stores no answers. Read the product limits.
