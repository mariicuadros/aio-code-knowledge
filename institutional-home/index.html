Our approach

Traceability. Interoperability. Responsible control.

DEOS connects entity identity, documented evidence and authorized operations while keeping source records, approval workflows and publication boundaries distinct. Human review remains essential for sensitive records.

Future development

Towards an agent-ready digital entity ecosystem.

Our roadmap aims to evolve the current administrative console into a DEOS application with managed client spaces, interoperable records, scoped agent permissions, human oversight, revocation mechanisms and action history. These are development objectives, not deployed external agent controls.

IP & legal documentation

Organized records for cross-border professional review.

DEOS is being developed to help creators and organizations organize authorship and ownership claims, creative-work provenance, contracts, consent records, permissions, dates, sources and related evidence in a traceable dossier. Its planned international scope includes mapping documentation requirements relevant to intellectual property and applicable local frameworks, subject to jurisdiction-specific professional review.

SCOPE & LIMITS

AIO CODE is a technology venture, not a law firm, legal representative, rights registry or certification authority in any country. It does not provide legal advice, determine ownership, register intellectual property, guarantee cross-border legal compliance or replace qualified counsel. DEOS can help prepare organized records for a client's independent lawyer or specialist.
