## Five-minute spoken English script

**0:00–0:35 — Introduction**
Hello, thank you for the opportunity to introduce AIO CODE. My name is Israel, and I support its technical development as co-founder. AIO CODE was created by Marii Cuadros, an independent researcher and creator, to address a problem she experienced directly: a creator's identity, projects, works, permissions and online representations become fragmented across platforms.

**0:35–1:20 — The problem**
Creators increasingly work across social platforms, brands, AI-assisted production tools, and digital characters. Their identities, contributions and rights-related documents are rarely managed together. When a client, studio or AI-powered tool needs to understand who made something and what they are authorized to do with it, that information is often difficult to locate and evaluate.

**1:20–2:15 — Our solution**
We are building AIO CODE, a Digital Entity Operating System for creators. It connects canonical identities, aliases and relationships to creative works, provenance records, permissions and supporting evidence. We also observe how external AI systems retrieve and describe those entities. Our principle is identity before visibility and evidence before conclusions. AIO CODE is not a computer operating system and it does not control external AI models.

**2:15–3:15 — What is built**
We have a documented entity graph and canonical passports, structured public metadata, a provenance and evidence architecture, a local public-source retrieval prototype, and an AI observation framework. We have developed an early Entity Console and entity API with local tests, revision control and approval gates. We can demonstrate the administrative flow with fictional data. We are in Phase 2 and still validating secure remote integration, commercial usability and pilot outcomes.

**3:15–4:05 — Who we serve and commercial opportunity**
Our starting market is independent creators and artists, especially those managing multiple identities, characters or AI-assisted works. We also want to collaborate with brands, talent representatives, music labels, studios and creative businesses. Potential commercial models include managed onboarding and future subscription tools for creator catalogs, evidence and permissions. These models still require customer validation.

**4:05–4:40 — Future vision**
Our long-term vision is portable creator identity and permissions: records that can interoperate with content provenance standards, verifiable credentials, licensing workflows and eventually authorized AI agents. Such agents would need explicit scopes, review and audit logs. We do not offer automatic arbitration, global rights certification, guaranteed AI citations or royalties from external systems.

**4:40–5:00 — Ask**
We are looking for technical and commercial partners, opportunities to validate pilot workflows, and guidance on how to develop interoperable creator infrastructure. We would welcome connections to the creative-tech and digital-economy ecosystem.

## Expected questions & responsible answers
**Is it a finished SaaS?** No. It is a Phase 2 operating architecture with tested components and a local prototype. Multi-tenant operations are not yet in production.
**Do you guarantee AI recognition or ranking?** No. We record observations of third-party systems, with dates and evidence.
**Are you a legally incorporated company?** OZCU is the declared developer venture; formal legal registration is pending.
**Do you register copyright or certify authorship?** No. We organize evidence and rights-related information; relevant rights and enforcement depend on applicable laws, agreements and independent verification.
**Are agents working?** Not as a commercial service. AgentAuthorization is an architectural and controlled-simulation direction.
**What do you need?** Pilot partners, feedback from representatives/studios and technical or commercialization guidance.
**Why not just C2PA or a GEO dashboard?** We connect identity, provenance and rights-related information into a workflow for creators, and separately observe external AI representation. We do not claim to replace either standard or tool.
**What evidence validates demand?** Not yet established; the purpose of external pilots and interviews is to test willingness to pay and workflow value.
