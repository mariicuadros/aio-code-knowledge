## 5. Prohibited uses

Unless separately and lawfully agreed, the participant's identity may not be used for:
- sexual/intimate or pornographic content;
- defamatory, humiliating or discriminatory content;
- fraudulent or deceptive impersonation;
- false criminal/illegal acts or false factual statements attributed as real;
- political/electoral persuasion;
- medical/financial endorsements;
- unrelated third-party licensing;
- any use materially outside the informed purpose of this agreement.

## 6. Voice / name / public identity

Natural voice and public/professional name may be used only where expressly necessary for the selected project purposes.

Persistent voice cloning or reusable synthetic voice requires separate consent.

## 7. Personal-data processing

The participant authorizes the processing of personal data strictly necessary to execute, document and prove this agreement and the authorized project uses. Sensitive/biometric processing, when applicable, will be limited to the necessary purpose and subject to access/security controls.

## 8. Technology providers

Necessary data may be processed by selected hosting, editing, signature, publication and AI technology providers to carry out an authorized purpose. The project should minimize disclosure and use restrictive settings where reasonably available.
