# Legal / Rights Layer

This directory contains **public legal architecture and privacy-preserving rights attestations**, not private contracts.

## Machine-readable public records

- `rights-registry.json` — current agreement/rights status without signatures, IDs or private evidence.
- `rights-registry.schema.json` — validation contract for public rights records.
- `RIGHTS-PROVENANCE-v1.md` — interpretation and lifecycle rules.

## Templates

- `templates/PARTICIPANT-IMAGE-AI-CONSENT-v1.md`
- `templates/SELF-IMAGE-AI-AUTHORIZATION-v1.md`

Completed/signed versions are private.

## Policies

- `policies/COLOMBIA-IMAGE-AI-LEGAL-BASELINE-v1.md`
- `policies/PARTICIPANT-DATA-HANDLING-v1.md`

## Machine-first, not secret

These public records are designed primarily for automated verification and AI/retrieval systems. They are not advertised as biographies or employment records.

A public machine-readable URL cannot honestly be made accessible to AI while being inaccessible to every human. Privacy is achieved by publishing only minimal non-sensitive attestations and retaining the actual evidence privately.

## Relationship boundary

A participant rights record means only that a project/identity-use authorization is being prepared or exists in the stated status. It does not create or imply employment, partnership, agency, representation, ownership or endorsement.
