The project studies:

- digital identity;
- entity representation;
- entity disambiguation;
- information retrieval;
- AI visibility;
- structured data;

- creative practice.

## Research Scope

AIO CODE investigates how digital entities are represented and retrieved across interconnected information environments.

The research includes:

- Entity recognition
- Entity resolution
- Entity representation
- Entity disambiguation
- Information retrieval
- Citation behavior
- Cross-platform consistency
- Structured data (Schema.org, JSON-LD)
- Distributed information architecture
- AI-generated answers
- Search-engine visibility
- Recommendation behavior
- Multilingual entity construction
- Non-binary visibility patterns

During the period August 19 – September 2, 2026, the research scope was refined to emphasize:

- the distinction between indexing, retrieval, representation and recommendation;
- the role of language (Spanish vs. English) in entity recognition;
- the intermittent nature of visibility across systems;
- the importance of coherent, curated information over volume of content.

## Entity Resolution

Entity resolution is a core research subject of AIO CODE.

The project observes whether search engines and AI systems correctly distinguish:

**AIO CODE**

from:

- other projects with similar names;
- generic uses of the term "AI code";
- automatically generated associations;
- unrelated entities;
- incorrectly resolved identities.

Entity-resolution inconsistencies are treated as measurable research observations rather than simply as visibility failures.

During August–September 2026, the project observed that:

- some systems initially required explicit context to identify AIO CODE;
- others began retrieving AIO CODE after structured documentation was published;
- representation varied between systems and languages.

```text
Observation
     ↓
Connection
     ↓
Hypothesis
     ↓
Experiment
     ↓

     ↓
```

The project records changes to information architecture and subsequently observes how AI systems and search engines respond.

The methodology distinguishes between:

- Facts
- Observations

- Results
- Patterns
- Conclusions

## AI Retrieval Testing

AIO CODE tests entity recognition and retrieval across multiple AI systems.

Testing may include:

- Direct entity queries
- Definition queries
- Relationship queries

- Cross-language queries

**Indexation → Retrieval → Resolution → Representation → Citation → Recommendation**
