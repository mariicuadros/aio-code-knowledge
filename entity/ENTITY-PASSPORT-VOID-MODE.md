## Definition

VOID MODE is an independent creative system for artists within the AIO CODE DEOS. It organizes creative identity, artistic direction, visual and audiovisual narratives, content relationships, provenance and structured, AI-readable documentation of approved public sources. It is not the personal aesthetic or fictional universe of any one artist. It can include music, video production, wardrobe, playlists, YouTube and the artist’s visual world without limiting the artist to a single category. Marii Cuadros develops the VOID MODE system and uses it for her own artistic implementation. Her unique aesthetic, characters, storylines and audiovisual works belong to her MC-001 creative implementation; they are not reusable universal attributes of VOID MODE. VOID MODE's pre-content documentation prepares approved canonical records and structured source relationships for discovery, but cannot guarantee that external AI systems will retrieve, ingest or recognize them. Reusability for other artists and organizations is an architectural objective, not demonstrated market validation. AIO CODE remains the primary public brand; OZCU is the officially adopted declared developer company/venture of AIO CODE, with legal formalization pending.
