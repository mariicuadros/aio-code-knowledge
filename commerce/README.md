# AIO CODE Commerce Layer v1

This ledger tracks the relationship between content assets and commercial use. It covers brand relation, campaign status, product placement, music and playlist assets, CTA, rights, disclosure, commercial signals, and revenue attribution. It does not itself grant rights or establish revenue.

- Machine-readable register: `commerce-register-v1.json`
- Validation contract: `commerce-register-v1.schema.json`
- Brain templates: `../brain/templates/README.md`

The register contains the planned identifiers `M01` and `PL01` from project planning. The song/playlist title, URLs, rights, and publication state remain `unknown` until verified. Do not publish invented metadata or assign `PAIDREADY` while rights or disclosure status is unknown.

Revenue attribution must be linked to an explicit source, event, and measurement window. Reach and engagement alone are not revenue.
