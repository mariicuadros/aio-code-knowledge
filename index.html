A connected operating system for digital entities

AIO CODE is a Digital Entity Operating System created by Marii Cuadros. It connects canonical identity, relationships, evidence, content provenance and dated platform observations into a continuing operating cycle.

Its distinctive contribution is how these components work together. A person's identity, a brand's public claims, the origin of a published asset and its supporting documentation remain connected across records and platforms. Teams can follow a record back to its source and update it as the entity evolves.

Phase 1 established the infrastructure and documented external search and AI results for the first case. Phase 2 applies that foundation to content operations, authorized analytics and additional entities.

Connected evidence for companies working with AI

AIO CODE addresses a practical information problem: identity, public claims, content origins, permissions and platform results are spread across profiles, files and teams. The system connects them around the digital entity they describe.

Its records connect canonical identities and relationships, claims with supporting sources, publication URLs and dates, and available provenance, permission and contract documentation. Dated observations preserve how external systems describe the entity over time.

This gives brand, content and AI governance teams a traceable basis for review: who the record concerns, where the information came from, which documents support it, and what needs to be updated before it is used again.

Documentation people can take to a lawyer

AIO CODE's evidence and provenance structure helps people and companies assemble the factual material for professional review: identity records, sources, publication history, content origin, and the permissions or contract references they provide.

Connecting those records makes it possible to present a documented account of a digital entity and its content to a lawyer, rights specialist or governance team. They can examine the sources, identify missing documentation and assess the rights and obligations relevant to the case.

This documentation role is a practical benefit of the system. AIO CODE organizes evidence; legal professionals assess its legal significance. Recording a permission or contract does not validate its legal effect or certify compliance.

Infrastructure that evolves with search and AI

AIO CODE combines structured data, canonical entity records, versioned sources, public publishing and observations of search and AI responses. Its infrastructure includes Vercel, Blogger, GitHub, Hugging Face, JSON-LD, sitemaps, a controlled public evidence search and an implemented IndexNow submission workflow.

IndexNow notifies participating search engines when a public URL changes. AIO CODE uses that mechanism alongside its existing publishing and evidence records. Submission acceptance and subsequent indexing are recorded as separate events; the Bing outcome following the latest sitemap update remains pending.

AI-assisted search is already part of the platform environment: Meta documents web search within Meta AI. AIO CODE tracks changes in that environment and incorporates relevant tools into its workflows through implementation, testing and dated observation.

A continuing operating cycle

Define the entity → Preserve the baseline → Publish and connect sources → Observe external results → Compare changes → Document findings → Update the entity.

Each change is connected to its date, source and observation so the system retains its history as new content, integrations and entities are added.
