One connected system

Your identity, sources and content need to stay connected.

A profile describes you. A publication records your work. A permission document explains an authorized use. Search and AI systems may represent you differently over time. AIO CODE connects these records around the entity they concern, preserving their sources and history.

01 · Identity

Define the entity

Canonical names, aliases, roles, official profiles and explicit relationships form a coherent reference for the person, brand or project.

02 · Evidence

Connect each claim to its source

Versioned records connect statements, publication URLs, dates and available content-origin, permission and contract documentation.

03 · Observation

Keep a history of what changes

Dated observations preserve how search and AI interfaces describe an entity, so changes can be examined against their evidence.

Current capabilities

A documented foundation, with connected operations expanding in Phase 2.

Phase 2 expands practical implementation through authorized platform integrations, content records and connected operations. Research and measurement protocols are internal operating procedures of the DEOS.

Implemented foundation
  • Entity passports, canonical records and relationship mapping.
  • Claim Ledger and rules connecting claims to evidence.
  • Controlled public evidence search with source and version references.
  • Public entity pages, structured data, sitemaps and an IndexNow submission workflow.
  • Contracts and registries for content, provenance, rights documentation and performance records.
  • Dated search and AI observations for the primary case.
Phase 2 · Integration work
  • Meta/Instagram Preview reads have been observed for identity, media and account Insights.
  • Production collection, private vault continuity and the dashboard flow still require end-to-end confirmation.
  • The private Entity Console API remains a draft integration.
  • YouTube and TikTok connectors, Content Genome population and AI reasoning over verified records remain development workstreams.

Public documentation and authorized private operations have separate access boundaries. Credentials are kept outside the repositories.

The pilot workflow

A clear scope. A traceable record. A next step you can act on.

Each delivery builds on authorized information and preserves its sources. Public references can support discovery; private evidence remains available to the people authorized to review it.

  1. Agree on the entity and scopeIdentify the subject, priorities, information permissions and deliverables.
  2. Organize identity and evidenceConnect canonical records, official sources, content and supporting documents.
  3. Prepare implementationPrioritize approved corrections, source connections and publishing actions.
  4. Observe and reportRecord external results, distinguish findings from missing data and document the next intervention.
Evidence & provenance

Documentation that supports professional review.

Identity records, content origin, publication history and available permission or contract references can be organized into a traceable dossier for a rights specialist, lawyer or governance team to review.

Recording a document preserves its reference and context. Its legal significance is assessed by the appropriate professional; AIO CODE does not certify ownership or legal compliance.
