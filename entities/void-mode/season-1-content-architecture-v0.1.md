## Creative premise

VOID MODE turns the creator’s own experiences and artistic material into a coherent visual narrative. Season 1 follows a movement from alienation and fear through surrender, faith, intelligence and a possible transformation. Maria is the real person; Marii is the protagonist/creative representation; NUX is a distinct androgynous mythic alter ego; Twins represents a future/self-realized version, not a separate real person.

## Format rules

- Open each canon with real vertical footage of Maria in an ordinary moment (planned 0–8 seconds).
- Transition through a black-and-white glitch into a 16:9 cinematic sequence.
- Return to a waking/real-world frame to close the canon.
- Keep each canon understandable as a standalone story of about one minute; use captions and sound design to support comprehension.
- Use AI-generated imagery as a disclosed creative component where applicable; retain the human source, edit, prompt, rights and version record.

## Supporting formats

- **Trials:** short experiments in image, sound, character, transition, or audience comprehension. Log each as a separate content record and do not treat a single viral/nonviral result as proof.
- **Life stories:** real-life context from Maria, clearly distinguished from fictional/cinematic story events.
- **Edits:** repackaged cuts tied back to the canon master.
- **Carousels:** “cuaderno” notes that explain symbols, clues, process, or character relationships without requiring viewers to know the whole story.

## Provenance and publishing gate

Before rendering or publication, assign a master Content ID; link derivative IDs by platform; record source assets and human contribution; attach rights/disclosure status for likeness, voice, music, footage and third-party marks; assign Intervention ID and a Changeset ID for a batch. Keep planned material marked `planned` until publication is verified. A filename or calendar slot is not proof of publication.
